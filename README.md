# OpenZero

Your data, in your hands.

**Live site:** https://buharialiyuai.github.io/openzero/

## What is OpenZero?

OpenZero helps people prove who they are without revealing their personal data.

## Parts of OpenZero

- **OpenZero ID:** Prove who you are, without revealing your secrets.
- **OpenZero Wallet:** Store your credentials and assets. Only you hold the key.
- **OpenZero AI:** An assistant that works for you, with only the access you allow.
